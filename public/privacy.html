Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the GenAI Foundations learning platform ("we", "us", "the app") handles your information when you sign in with Google.

Information we collect

  • Google account email — used to identify you and assign your role (student, instructor, or admin).
  • Display name — the name associated with your Google account, shown to you in the app header.
  • Learning progress — which modules you've marked complete and any checkpoint responses you submit. This is stored only in your browser's local storage — not on any server.

How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and show your name in the app.
  • To determine your role based on your email address.
  • To display your progress to you (and, for instructor/admin emails, to display aggregate cohort progress).

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect passwords, payment information, or location data.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Your learning progress never leaves your browser — it is not transmitted to any server.

Authentication via Google

When you click "Sign in with Google", Google authenticates you and shares your email and display name with this app via a signed ID token. We decode this token to read your email and name. We do not send your data back to Google.

Data storage

  • Session: Your sign-in session is stored in your browser's sessionStorage and is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Progress: Stored in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Server storage: None. This app has no backend database.

Your rights

  • You may sign out at any time — your session is cleared.
  • You may clear your progress by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.
  • You may use the app without signing in by contacting the instructor for alternative access.
